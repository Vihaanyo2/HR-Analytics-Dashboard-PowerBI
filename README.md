 HR Analytics Dashboard | Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

This HR Analytics Dashboard was developed using **Power BI** to help HR teams analyze workforce trends, monitor employee attrition, and identify factors affecting employee retention. The dashboard provides interactive visualizations and KPIs that enable data-driven HR decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

Organizations often struggle to understand why employees leave and which departments or employee groups are most affected by attrition. HR teams need an interactive dashboard to monitor workforce metrics and identify patterns that impact employee retention.

---

## 🎯 Project Objective

The objective of this project is to:

- Monitor employee attrition
- Analyze workforce demographics
- Compare attrition across departments and age groups
- Measure the impact of overtime on attrition
- Track key HR KPIs
- Support HR managers with data-driven insights

---

## 📂 Dataset

**Dataset:** IBM HR Analytics Employee Attrition & Performance Dataset

The dataset contains employee information such as:

- Employee Number
- Age
- Gender
- Department
- Job Role
- Monthly Income
- Attrition
- OverTime
- Education
- Marital Status
- Years at Company
- Job Satisfaction

---

## 📌 Key Business Insights

- Research & Development department has the highest employee attrition.
- Employees aged **26–35** experience the highest attrition.
- Employees working overtime have higher attrition compared to those who do not.
- Managers receive the highest average monthly income.
- The dashboard enables HR teams to monitor employee trends and make informed decisions.
